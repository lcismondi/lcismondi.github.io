01 / HVAC · PRODUCT AND OPERATIONS

RENEW THE PRODUCT.
TRANSFORM HOW
IT IS BUILT.

Renewing controls, digital fabrication, processes and the team. Working directly with a manufacturer's leadership to connect product development and operations.

Responsibilities, decisions and outcomes
Context
A manufacturing business with slow growth and a product range and infrastructure in need of renewal.
Challenge
Modernize HVAC controls and development and production tools, coordinating products, facilities and the team.
My role
Working directly with management and leading the renewal of products, infrastructure and processes.
Actions
Renew the thermostat range and introduce in-house prototyping, standardized processes and new production tools.
Architecture
Integrating electronics and touch or capacitive interfaces with product design, prototypes and injection molds.
Outcome
Renewed the range with touchscreens, larger LCDs and backlit capacitive buttons. Created a digital fabrication workshop with 3D printing, laser cutting and UV printing.
Learning and impact
Completely refurbished the warehouse with coded storage locations, modernized production tools and strengthened the team to help new staff learn.

02 / REFRIGERATION · PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

FROM THE FIRST
PROTOTYPE TO
PRODUCTION.

Co-founding and leading the technology behind a beverage-cooling product: prototyping, validation, early funding rounds and international industrialization.

Responsibilities, decisions and outcomes
Context
Packaged beverage consumption in retail and public spaces.
Challenge
Combine rapid cooling and the right drinking temperature with efficient energy use.
My role
Co-founder and CTO: built the prototype from scratch, validated the concept and contributed to funding and market research.
Actions
Connect technical development with early funding rounds, research and market testing to move toward industrial production.
Architecture
Integrating hardware, control, mechanics and AI to cool packaged beverages.
Outcome
Validated the concept and took development through to international industrialization during my involvement.
Learning and impact
The foundations established during development supported the product's subsequent expansion into international markets.
